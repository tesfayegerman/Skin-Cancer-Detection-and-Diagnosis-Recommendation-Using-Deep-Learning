Know Your Skin Health

Understanding skin cancer can be life-saving. Here's everything you need to know.

What is Skin Cancer?

Skin cancer is the abnormal growth of skin cells, most often developing on skin exposed to the sun — but it can also occur on areas that don't get much sun exposure. There are several types, including basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and the most dangerous form, melanoma. It is the world's most diagnosed cancer, with early detection dramatically improving treatment outcomes. The good news: when identified early, the vast majority of skin cancers are highly treatable.

Common Symptoms

  • Unusual mole or new skin growth
  • Changes in size, color, or shape
  • Sores that won't heal
  • Irregular, ragged borders
  • Itching, bleeding, or tenderness
  • Pearly, waxy, or scaly bumps

Known Causes

  • UV radiation from sun exposure
  • Tanning beds and UV lamps
  • Fair skin and light-colored eyes
  • Family or personal history of cancer
  • Weakened immune system
  • Exposure to radiation or chemicals

Early Detection

When caught early, skin cancer — including melanoma — has a 5-year survival rate of over 99% for localized cases. Advanced stage melanoma drops to under 30%. AI screening tools like DermAI are designed to flag concerns early, prompting timely professional consultation.

Prevention Tips

  • Apply SPF 30+ sunscreen daily
  • Avoid UV exposure 10AM–4PM
  • Wear hats and protective clothing
  • Avoid tanning beds entirely
  • Perform monthly self-examinations
  • Annual dermatologist screening

Deep Learning Model

Built on ResNet50 — pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on dermoscopic images. It detects subtle visual patterns invisible to the naked eye, including texture anomalies, color gradients, and structural irregularities that distinguish benign from malignant lesions.

📊

HAM10000 Dataset

Trained on the Human Against Machine (HAM10000) benchmark — over 10,000 dermatoscopic images of common pigmented skin lesions, annotated by expert dermatologists. This ensures the model learns from real, validated clinical data.

How It Works

Upload a clear image of your skin lesion → our model preprocesses it to 224×224 pixels → ResNet50 extracts deep visual features → a softmax classifier outputs probabilities for all 7 lesion classes → results and recommendations are displayed instantly.
